Group Matters

A place where a group keeps track of the matters it needs to answer together, links each one to the tool where it's being worked on, and records how it was answered.

Spaces on this server are reached through a secret link. If you're part of a group using it, ask your group for the link.

Privacy

No accounts, no trackers, no analytics, no third-party scripts, no cookies. Your group's matters, links and the display names people type are stored on this server, and anyone with a space's link can read them. Read the full privacy notice.
