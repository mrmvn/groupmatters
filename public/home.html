Group Matters

A place where a group keeps track of the matters it needs to answer together, links each one to the tool where it's being worked on, and records how it was answered.

Spaces on this server are reached through a secret link. If you're part of a group using it, ask your group for the link.

Privacy

  • We store what your group enters: matters, links, and the display names people type (not verified), plus an activity log recording who changed what and when. Anyone with a space's view or edit link can read all of it, names included.
  • No accounts, no trackers, no analytics, no third-party scripts. Your browser keeps your display name and a few preferences locally.
  • When a matter links to a public page (GitHub, Loomio…), the server fetches that page to show its status.
  • This server is run by the operator who gave your group its link. To see, export or remove your data, or to have a name removed, ask them (or your group).
